## BE language differences relevant to localization
- BE/NL relies more on `werkblad` and `keukenblad`; do not mechanically import NL `aanrechtblad` usage.
- BE/FR category language is built around `plan de travail` and must be validated against current local search context.
- Market term balance is not a translation task. The localizer must re-evaluate term choice per market.

## Interpretation rules
- Search volume is not business outcome.
- High-volume demand for a material CanDo does not sell must be answered honestly, never converted into a false claim.
- `op maat` is a high-intent route, not the whole category story.
- These numbers are frozen benchmark data, not live estimates.
