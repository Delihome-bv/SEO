# Product Truth — CanDo Worktops Made to Measure
Freeze: 2026-09-15
This file owns product truth for the benchmark. Anything not CONFIRMED is not publishable as fact.

## Shared proposition
Three series: Essential, Advance, Signature. Essential and Advance share the same 25 decor names. Signature has 8 own finishes.

## Essential — CONFIRMED
- 38 mm.
- 25 decors.
- chipboard core.
- HPL top layer.
- straight edge.
- up to 4000 mm long.
- up to 1200 mm deep.
- cut-outs can be prepared for tap, hob and top-mounted sink.
- customer uses/buys a top-mounted sink separately.
- FSC applies to the chipboard core.
- current existing-line price data exists internally, but no consumer price claim is permitted here.
Do not extrapolate Essential values to Advance or Signature.

## Advance — CONFIRMED unless OPEN
Consumer wording: `een lichte kern met een dragend raster`.
Technical wording: `MDF met gridstructuur`.
- HPL surface.
- 25 decors, same names as Essential.
- 32 mm and 38 mm.
- straight edge.
- flush-mounted sink possible.
- sink can be selected/ordered.
- tap cut-out and hob cut-out can be prepared.
- FSC and CARB2 supported for this benchmark.
- lighter construction is mainly relevant for larger worktops and islands.
FORBIDDEN: honeycomb/hexagonal cells, recycled wood fibre as raw-material claim, claims that the core changes HPL scratch/heat resistance, consumer price, delivery time, exact maximum size.
OPEN: exact maximum size; final delivery time.

## Signature — CONFIRMED unless OPEN
- solid composite based on recycled glass and resin.
- no separate core/top layer.
- 12 mm standard thickness.
- 8 finishes: Polished = Wolk Wit, Kristal Wit, Parel Grijs, Nevel Grijs; Velvet = Vanille Creme, Zand Beige, Klei Taupe, Nacht Zwart.
- faceted edge; do not communicate a 30 mm visible-face construction.
- flush-mounted sink possible.
- sink can be selected/ordered.
- tap cut-out and hob cut-out can be prepared.
- PITT cooking supported.
- non-porous.
- no impregnation required.
- avoid bleach and ammonia.
- use a trivet for hot pans.
FORBIDDEN: `100% gerecycled werkblad`, `100% gerecycled materiaal`, `volledig recyclebaar`, consumer price, delivery time.
OPEN: final delivery time.

## Sink and tap rule — freeze 15-09-2026
- Essential: no sink supplied through this proposition; top-mounted sink bought separately.
- Advance and Signature: sink may be selected/ordered.
- taps are NOT supplied/ordered at launch.
- tap hole / tap cut-out may still be specified and prepared.
Never write that the customer orders `spoelbak én kraan` together.

## Precision work
Safe wording: `Het precieze zaag- en freeswerk wordt vooraf gedaan op basis van de opgegeven maten en gekozen uitsparingen.`
Do not claim every imaginable cut-out, zero drilling/frezer work in every situation, or zero adjustment in all cases.

## Installation
Self-placement is part of the proposition. Advise working with two people. No fixed installation-time claim.

## Price
No price anchor or `vanaf` price on the consumer page. Multiple current price levels exist and Advance/Signature lack confirmed prices. Price is shown in the retailer flow.

## Delivery
No generic delivery promise. No delivery time for Advance or Signature.

## Material names
Never publish supplier/material trade names for Advance core or Signature material.
