# Audience & Decision Context — Worktops Made to Measure
Status: CONFIRMED.

## Broad brand audience
DIY consumers who want to improve their home themselves or with some help. Historical brand persona includes Roos & Ben, a younger household looking for inspiration and clear help. Do not write exclusively for one age group.

## Primary worktop decision segments
### Do it together — PRIMARY
Wants a made-to-measure result, is willing to place/install the worktop, does not want to gamble on measurement or precision cutting. Needs reassurance, series choice, clear process and confidence that precision work is handled before delivery.

### Do it for me — SECONDARY
Wants the result more than the technical process and may doubt whether installation is manageable. Needs simplicity and expectation management. There is no confirmed installation service in this benchmark, so do not promise one.

### Do it yourself — SECONDARY/EDGE
More confident DIY user who may also consider a standard worktop and self-cutting. Needs factual comparison and a clear reason for made-to-measure.

## Decision state
Mainly evaluating. The page must answer why made to measure, which series fits, what can be prepared in advance, what the customer still does and where to continue.

## Writing implication
Reassure and help choose. Do not dump a specification catalogue. Do not split the three series into three isolated SEO pages.
