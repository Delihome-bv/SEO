# SEO & GEO Rules — Benchmark

## SEO
Preserve owner URL. Do not create another competing landing page. Use primary keyword naturally in title/H1/body. Use wider category vocabulary where relevant: werkblad, keukenblad, aanrechtblad. Internal links must exist now or be `VERIFY_LIVE`. Redirecting/excluded/dead URLs may not be used. Write for the decision, not keyword stuffing.

## GEO / AI readability
Answer-first paragraphs. Factual paragraphs must be understandable without context. Put comparable facts in semantic tables where the stramien allows. Keep product facts source-backed. FAQ answers self-contained. Do not claim AI-search performance as proven business impact.

## Metadata
Deliver meta title, meta description, H1 and suggested schema types compatible with page type.

## FAQ
Use 4–6 questions that add decision value. Do not repeat the body word-for-word.

## Schema
Use only schema matching visible page content. No fake ratings, reviews, availability or price.
