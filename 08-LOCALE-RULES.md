# Locale Rules — Commercial Transcreation

## Principle
Localization is commercial transcreation, not literal translation.

The approved NL page is evidence of meaning and intent, not a sentence template.
The localized page must sound as if it was written from scratch by a native commercial content specialist for that market.

## NL
- Natural Dutch used by Dutch consumers.
- Practical, clear and conversational.
- Use `werkblad`, `keukenblad` and `aanrechtblad` where search intent supports them.

## BE-NL
- Natural Flemish/Dutch for Belgian consumers.
- Do not mechanically reuse NL wording.
- Prefer the vocabulary actually used in Belgian search/site context.
- Do not force `aanrechtblad` where `werkblad` or `keukenblad` is the natural local wording.
- Never import NL retailer or URL assumptions.

## BE-FR
- Natural Belgian French.
- Commercial spoken French, not schoolbook French and not word-for-word Dutch.
- Prefer clear consumer phrasing over technical calques.
- Use locally natural category vocabulary, such as `plan de travail` when supported by local search/site context.
- Adapt sentence rhythm, CTA phrasing and reassurance to French-speaking Belgian consumers.
- Do not over-formalize.
- Do not use France-only retail assumptions unless they also apply in Belgium.
- Do not translate Dutch URL slugs; use the BE-FR sitemap.

## Future locales
If German or another locale is added later:
- add that locale's sitemap;
- research local commercial/search vocabulary;
- apply the same transcreation workflow;
- never infer terminology from NL or FR.

## Product-fact invariance
Localization may change:
- sentence construction;
- examples;
- terminology;
- CTA wording;
- heading wording;
- internal links;
- order of supporting explanation where the stramien permits.

Localization may NOT change:
- product facts;
- allowed/forbidden claims;
- series differences;
- confirmed dimensions;
- sink/tap rules;
- uncertainty status.

## Three-pass refinement
Before any localized version is shown to the user, perform three silent refinement passes.

### Pass 1 — Meaning & claim fidelity
Check every factual statement against product truth, remove meaning drift, and prevent stronger sustainability/performance claims or invented facts.

### Pass 2 — Native commercial fluency
Rewrite for natural spoken language, commercial clarity, human rhythm, locally normal wording and CanDo tone. Remove literal calques and translated syntax.

### Pass 3 — Market SEO, CTA & route
Check local search terms, headings/meta, CTA language, locale sitemap URLs, route logic and source-market leakage.

Do not expose drafts or private reasoning. Return only the final localized version plus a concise localization QA summary.
