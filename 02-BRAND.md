# CanDo Brand — Benchmark Extract
Status: CONFIRMED.

## Brand promise
CanDo helps people make their home more beautiful themselves, in a way that feels achievable and affordable.
Pay-off: `You CanDo it`

## Brand character
Helpful, practical, result-oriented, optimistic without hype, personal, approachable and confidence-building.
Not luxury/premium theatre, not technical for the sake of technicality, not shouty, not vague lifestyle language, not a professional-contractor brand.

## Voice
Write like a capable friend who makes the job feel manageable.
Use plain Dutch, explain choices before specifications, translate technique into customer meaning, avoid exaggerated certainty and unnecessary jargon, and help the reader decide and move forward.

## Worktops made to measure
Reduce uncertainty about measuring, fear of precision cutting/frezer work, uncertainty about material/finish and uncertainty about which series fits the use case.
Desired feeling: `Ik snap de verschillen, ik weet wat bij mijn keuken past en ik weet wat mijn volgende stap is.`

## CTA tone
Action-oriented, low-friction and concrete. Preferred pattern: `Stel je werkblad samen`.
No pressure, scarcity or unsupported savings language.
